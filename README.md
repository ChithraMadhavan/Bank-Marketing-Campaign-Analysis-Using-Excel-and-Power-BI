**Bank Marketing Campaign Analysis using Excel and Power BI**

The main purpose of this project is to analyze bank marketing data using Excel and Power BI in order to understand customer behavior and identify factors that influence subscription to a term deposit. Thie dataset is highly relevant as it contains bank marketing campaign data along with customer demographics and economic indicators. It helps analyze customer behavior and identify factors influencing subscription to term deposits.

**Key Metrics**
Total Customers
Subscription Count
Average Call Duration
Euribor Rate Trend Analysis

**Features**
Interactive Power BI dashboard
Job-wise subscription analysis
Campaign intensity analysis across months
Contact method distribution analysis
Education distribution analysis
Economic trend analysis using Euribor rate
Interactive slicers and drill-down features

**Tools Used**
Microsoft Excel
Power BI

**Key Insights**
Job role significantly influences subscription behavior, with administrative and technician groups showing higher subscription rates.
Cellular contact method is more effective compared to telephone communication.
Campaign performance varies across different months, indicating seasonal trends.
Higher education groups show relatively better subscription response.
Longer call duration increases the probability of customer subscription.
Economic indicators such as Euribor rate and employment trends influence customer decisions.
